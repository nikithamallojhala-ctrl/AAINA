Some Context

The roots of Bollywood dance

Bollywood choreography borrows from several distinct traditions. Knowing where a move comes from makes it easier to understand why it looks the way it does.

क

Kathak

A classical North Indian storytelling style known for fast spins, precise footwork, and expressive hand gestures called mudras.

भ

Bhangra

A high-energy folk style from Punjab built around shoulder shrugs, powerful arm pumps, and sharp, percussive footwork.

ग

Garba

A circular folk dance from Gujarat, traditionally performed with rhythmic claps and sweeping arm turns around a center point.

फ

Filmi Fusion

The modern Bollywood blend — classical lines and folk energy layered with hip-hop, jazz, and freestyle for the camera.

The Mechanics

How the mirror works

01

Pick a routine

Choose Foundations, Expressive, or Power Combos below — each one pulls from a different part of real Bollywood vocabulary, from mudras to spin prep.

02

The mirror tracks you

MediaPipe's pose model reads 33 points on your body, 30 times a second, and measures the angles at your shoulders and elbows against the target.

03

Beat the clock

Each pose gives you a few seconds. Land it before time runs out to keep your streak alive — miss it and the mirror moves on without you.
