How it works

No app download, no account. Your camera feed never leaves your device — all the pose tracking happens locally in the browser.

01

Strike the pose

A target pose appears — a classic Bollywood shape like the matki balance or the chaiyya-chaiyya open arms. You've got a few seconds to match it.

02

The mirror tracks you

Google's MediaPipe pose model reads 33 points on your body, 30 times a second, and measures the angles at your shoulders and elbows.

03

Get your score

Your angles are compared to the target's. Nail it and your streak climbs; miss it and you'll see exactly which arm needs adjusting.
